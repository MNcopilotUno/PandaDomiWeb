Panda Domi

Share the moment, not your child.

A cosy red panda journal for your family’s first years. First steps, funny moments, the things they say: each one becomes a little panda card you can send to the family.

Meet the pandas

When your baby arrives, your panda gets a cub

Your big red panda lives in a cosy den in a quiet valley. On the day your baby is born, a tiny cub arrives too, and sleeps curled up in the moss.

Every moment you share leaves a keepsake

Share a moment and your panda puts something on the shelf of the den. Over the months, the den fills up with your family’s year.

  • First steps
  • A first smile
  • A first word
  • That made us laugh
  • What they said
  • Tried something new
  • Slept through the night
  • A sweet moment

When you decide they’re ready, the cub is theirs

Never because of a birthday: you choose the day. Your child plays the cub themselves, counting berries on the farm, reading little signs in the valley and hearing the owl’s bedtime story.

And one day, the cub builds its own den nearby

Like a real red panda, the cub grows up and moves into a den of its own in the same valley. Your two pandas visit each other.

Moments, not photos

Pick a card, add a few words, tap share. It’s with grandma before the moment is over, and there is nothing of your child in it but their words.

Our promise

  • No photo of your child. Ever.

    No faces, no voices, no uploads, no camera button. A card carries panda art and the words you choose.

  • It lives on your phone

    It works offline and stays on your own devices. There is no account and no server holding your family’s moments.

  • No ads, no tracking, no cookies

    This page loads nothing from anyone else, and the journal never will.

  • No guilt

    No streaks, no timers, nothing wilts while you’re away. After a week of no sleep, everything is still there.

  • A safe place for small hands

    When your child plays, they never see a shop, a link out or a message from a stranger. It never compares them with anyone either.

Follow along

The den is being built right now. There is nothing to sign up for and no email to give: add the news feed to any feed reader, or simply come back. Nothing is lost by being away.

Good night, little panda.
