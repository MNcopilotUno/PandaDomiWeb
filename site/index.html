A quiet valley to explain the idea

This page: a shy panda behind an old tree, the story of the big panda and the cub, and the promise. It loads nothing from anyone else.

Now · being finished

The free journal

The big panda’s den with the tiny cub. Pick a card, add a few words and share it as a picture, in three taps. A timeline of your moments, a keepsake for each one, and export whenever you like. It works offline and stays on your phone.

Peek at the early build

Started · this winter

More moments, and a farm for you

Thirty to forty cards shaped by what families really share, short animated cards, and a cub that grows with your moments. You also get your own big panda to play: plant bamboo and berries, water them, harvest and feed. Crops never wither and nothing is lost by being away.

Groundwork in place · 2027

The cub becomes theirs

The free learning game. A first session with the shy panda, counting to 20 with berries, a reading ladder from first sounds to a bedtime book, the owl’s story, and the pika who brings medicine. For you, a summary of what they did with no scores, and a screen-time limit where the panda simply falls asleep.

Later · 2027

Extras that help real red pandas

Optional, for grown-ups only, behind the parent lock: Panda Guardian, with a fixed 20% going to red panda conservation, signed conservation medals, treehouse rooms, adventure packs and a printed book of your year. The free core stays complete.

Later · second half of 2027

Visits in Panda Valley

Your panda and your child’s visit each other, then friends’ pandas through invite links that you share. Small gifts, a classroom mode, QR codes at zoo red panda enclosures, and Red Panda Day every September.

Later · after that

Further on

Czech, with reading by syllables. Apps for Android and iOS. Your own voice for the bedtime story. And a link to Memoria Domi, where your real photos stay on your own devices.

Our promise

  • No photo of your child. Ever.

    No faces, no uploads, no camera button, and the app never records your child’s voice. A card carries panda art and the words you choose.

  • It lives on your phone

    It works offline and stays on your own devices. There is no account and no server holding your family’s moments.

  • Free at its heart, forever

    The journal, your own panda’s farm and the whole learning game to 20 never cost anything. Extras come later and stay optional.

  • No ads, no tracking, no cookies

    This page loads nothing from anyone else, and the journal never will.

  • No guilt

    No streaks, no timers, nothing wilts while you’re away. After a week of no sleep, everything is still there.

  • A safe place for small hands

    When your child plays, they never see a shop, a link out or a message from a stranger. It never compares them with anyone either.

Follow along

There is nothing to sign up for and no email to give: add the news feed to any feed reader, or simply come back. Nothing is lost by being away.
